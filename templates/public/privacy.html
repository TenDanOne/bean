BrightBean Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Who operates BrightBean Studio

BrightBean Studio is an internal social media management application operated by our company in Mexico. For privacy questions, contact {{ contact_email }}.

Information we process

  • Account information needed to identify an authorized user and their BrightBean workspace.
  • Basic TikTok profile information returned after authorization, such as the account identifier, display name, avatar, and union identifier when provided.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens required to maintain an authorized connection.
  • Videos, captions, publishing status, and post identifiers when an authorized user uses the publishing workflow.

How we use information

We use this information only to authenticate authorized company users, maintain connected social accounts, publish approved content, and operate and secure the internal application. We do not sell this information, use it for advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored in encrypted database fields. Access is limited to authorized company users and administrators who need it to operate the internal application. No security measure can guarantee absolute security, but we use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards for this internal deployment.

Sharing and service providers

Information is sent to the selected social platform only when an authorized user requests a connected-account action. We may use infrastructure providers to host the application and database; those providers process information only to provide the infrastructure and under applicable access controls.

Retention and deletion

We retain information while the connected account or company workspace is active and as needed for operational and security records. An authorized user can disconnect a social account in BrightBean. To request deletion or ask a privacy question, contact {{ contact_email }}.

Changes

We may update this policy when the internal application or its data practices change. The latest version will remain available on this page.
