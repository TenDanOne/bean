How to revoke access and request deletion

You can revoke BEAN's access and ask us to delete your data as follows:

  • In BEAN: disconnect the account from Social accounts; its tokens are deleted.
  • Facebook / Instagram: Facebook > Settings > Apps and Websites > remove BEAN. Meta notifies us, BEAN disconnects the accounts linked to your user and deletes their tokens.
  • TikTok: TikTok > Settings > Manage apps (Security and permissions) > remove BEAN.
  • YouTube / Google: Google Account > Security > Third-party apps & services > remove BEAN.
  • Full history deletion: email {{ contact_email }} naming the connected account.

What is deleted

Disconnecting an account (or receiving Meta's notice) deletes its tokens. Full deletion of the publishing history, metrics and messages is handled by email: we reply within 20 business days and carry it out within the following 15 business days, unless a legal obligation requires keeping it.
