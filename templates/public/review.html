TikTok integration

Login Kit

Authorized company users sign in with TikTok and connect their Business or Creator account to a BrightBean workspace.

Content Posting API

BrightBean publishes video posts created by authorized users directly to their connected TikTok account using Direct Post.

Account control

Users can disconnect a TikTok account from BrightBean. OAuth tokens are stored in encrypted database fields.

Data and access

BrightBean uses basic TikTok profile information to identify a connected account and uses authorized OAuth tokens only to provide the requested publishing workflow. The application does not sell TikTok data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for their own purposes.
